# Gemini adapter notes

Map PRP's semantic effort requirement to the thinking controls supported by the selected Gemini model/runtime.

Guidance:

- keep Gemini-specific thinking settings outside PRP's normative protocol;
- use evaluation-informed effort/budget mappings rather than static assumptions;
- prefer low/no extended reasoning for deterministic routine work when supported;
- increase reasoning for high-ambiguity, multi-step, evidence-dependent, or consequential work;
- record model/version, settings, token/usage data when available, latency, and substantive outcome.

A PRP level is not a fixed Gemini thinking budget.
